# Dania's Table Website

Build a modern, elegant one-page website for a restaurant called "Dania's Table Restaurant & Bar" located in Budapest.

BUSINESS INFO:

- Name: Dania's Table Restaurant & Bar

- Cuisine: Hungarian / Contemporary Hungarian cuisine with small plates and tapas-style tastings

- Address: Izabella u. 27/A, 1077 Budapest

- Phone: 06 30 319 6031

- Hours: Open daily, closes 11 pm

- Price range: 4,000–6,000 Ft per person

- Services: Dine-in and Delivery

- Rating: 4.9 out of 5 stars, based on 345 Google reviews

DESIGN & TYPOGRAPHY:

- Warm, upscale, cozy European bistro aesthetic — think candlelit dining, exposed brick, and authentic Hungarian charm

- Use a restaurant-appropriate font pairing: an elegant serif for headings (e.g. "Playfair Display" or "Cormorant Garamond") paired with a clean, readable sans-serif for body text (e.g. "Lato" or "Jost")

- Color palette: deep burgundy, warm gold/mustard accents, cream/off-white background, charcoal text — evoking goulash, wine, and candlelight

- Generous whitespace, subtle fade-in animations on scroll, smooth section transitions

- Fully responsive for mobile and desktop

IMAGE PLACEHOLDERS:

I'll be uploading my own photos later, one at a time, so use clearly labeled placeholder images/divs for now (e.g. "Hero image — restaurant interior," "Dish photo — Marha Gulyás," "Dish photo — Napi Leves") so I can easily swap them out afterward. Structure the code so each placeholder is easy for me to locate and replace individually.

PAGE STRUCTURE:

1. Hero section — full-width image placeholder, restaurant name in elegant serif font, tagline like "Authentic Hungarian Flavors in the Heart of Budapest," a prominent "Reserve a Table" button, and the star rating (4.9 ★ from 345 reviews) displayed near the top for social proof

2. About section — warm, inviting paragraph about the restaurant's character: authentic, welcoming atmosphere, personal touch from the proprietor, known for both intimate small-plate tastings and full meals

3. Menu highlights — showcase popular dishes as cards with image placeholders:

   - Napi Leves (Soup of the Day)

   - Marha Gulyás (Beef Goulash)

   - Include a note that goulash and chicken paprikash are guest favorites

   - "View Full Menu" button (linking to a placeholder menu page or PDF)

4. Reviews/Testimonials section — display 3-4 real guest reviews as styled cards with reviewer name, star rating, and quote:

   - Mateus Martins (Local Guide): "We stopped by Dania's for lunch while passing nearby and were pleasantly surprised. The atmosphere was lovely — very welcoming and full of character, with an authentic feel."

   - Charles Burks (Local Guide): "The perfect spot to taste the best of Budapest. I wanted to do more smaller plate tastings as opposed to a larger meal and was happy to discover Dania's Table. The proprietor was the perfect guide."

   - Lean Belly: "A very quiet and great place. I tried Hungarian cuisine for the first time, and it's absolutely delicious! The goulash, dessert, and all the other dishes are simply wonderful!"

   Include a "See all 345 reviews on Google" link/button

5. Gallery section — grid of image placeholders (food, interior, vibe) ready for me to fill in

6. Location & Hours section — embedded map placeholder, address, phone number (clickable to call), hours (highlight "Open now, closes 11 pm"), and dine-in/delivery badges

7. Reservation/Contact section — simple reservation form (name, date, time, party size, contact info) and a call-to-action to call or message the restaurant directly

8. Footer — restaurant name, address, phone, social media icon placeholders, and price range note (4,000–6,000 Ft per person)

FUNCTIONALITY:

- Sticky navigation bar with smooth scroll to sections (Home, About, Menu, Reviews, Gallery, Location, Reserve)

- Mobile-friendly hamburger menu

- "Reserve a Table" button fixed/visible throughout

- Click-to-call phone number

- Subtle hover effects on menu cards and buttons

Keep the code clean and well-commented so I can easily locate image placeholders to swap in my own photos later.
